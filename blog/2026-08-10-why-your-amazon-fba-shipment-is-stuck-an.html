Why Your Amazon FBA Shipment Is Stuck And How To Get It Released Fast

August 10, 2026 · Estimated reading: 9–11 minutes

FBA sellers face the same CBP holds as any other importer — but with tighter storage budgets and Amazon removal timelines. An EXAM hold, a late ISF, or a missing bond can strand inventory for days. Here is what actually causes the delay and what to do before it happens.

Before we start: the patterns below are drawn from publicly available CBP instructions, FDA guidance, and importer feedback. Release timelines vary by port, commodity, and whether the importer of record responds with the requested documentation. Treat this as a planning reference, not a ruling on a specific entry.

The most common FBA-specific triggers

Amazon FBA shipments are not held at a separate regulatory track, but the operational pressure makes every hold more expensive. The most frequent causes:

  • Late or incomplete Importer Security Filing (ISF). CBP requires 10+2 data at least 24 hours before vessel loading. Missing or inaccurate ISF triggers penalties under 19 CFR Part 701 and can delay release. See cbp.gov/trade/basic-import-export/importer-security-filing-isf.
  • Missing or under-valued customs bond. A shipment without a valid continuous bond, or with a single-transaction bond that does not cover duties, taxes, and MPF, will not clear. CBP authority: 19 U.S.C. § 1603 and CBP bond guidance.
  • EXAM / physical inspection holds. High-risk country of origin, vague invoice descriptions (e.g., "electronics parts"), or unverified supplier history trigger intensive exams under 19 U.S.C. § 1499 and 19 CFR Part 151.
  • FDA prior notice or facility registration gaps. Food, supplements, cosmetics, and medical devices require prior notice and current facility registration. Missing either triggers an FDA hold under FD&C Act, 21 U.S.C. § 381 and FSMA.
  • AD/CVD exposure not declared. Undeclared antidumping or countervailing duty liability is one of the fastest ways to freeze an entry. CBP publishes AD/CVD orders at cbp.gov/trade/admission-customs-bond/antidumping.
  • Wood packaging (ISPM 15). Non-compliant pallets or crates trigger USDA/APHIS holds under 7 CFR Part 319 and ISPM 15.

Reasonable care checklist before you book

CBP holds importers to a "reasonable care" standard under 19 U.S.C. § 1484. If the data you submitted was wrong or incomplete, the hold is your responsibility even if your broker signed the entry. Before freight is loaded:

  1. Verify the HTS code. Use the ClearanceIQ HTS Lookup to confirm the 8-digit code and general duty rate. A wrong code can shift from "Free" to a 25%+ ad valorem rate and trigger a value re-examination.
  2. Confirm country of origin marking. Every article must be marked with its country of origin in English. Exception: tightly packed standard-shipping-unit displays may be exempt, but bulk cargo without marking is subject to retention or marking-at-importer expense.
  3. Check AD/CVD status. Search CBP's AD/CVD database by HTS code and country. If an order is active, the bond amount must cover the AD/CVD deposit as well as standard duties.
  4. File ISF 10+2 on time. The filing window is 24 hours before lading. A late ISF draws a $5,000 flat penalty per violation. If you use a freight forwarder, confirm in writing that they filed — and ask for the confirmation number.
  5. Validate your bond. Continuous bond holders should confirm their bond is active and the face amount covers the shipment. Single-transaction bond users should verify the bond amount matches the estimated duties, taxes, and fees (including MPF and HMF).

What to do when the shipment is already stuck

If you see a hold code in the ACE portal or your broker notifies you, follow this sequence:

  1. Get the exact hold code and requesting agency. Do not treat "customs hold" as a single condition. EXAM, PGA, and FDA hold release criteria are different.
  2. Do not re-book the same drayage appointment. If the hold requires a sample or CES exam, a second truck may be refused at the gate until the exam is complete.
  3. Respond to the data request within 72 hours. CBP and PGAs set clocks. Late responses convert a documentation hold into a liquidation or abandonment risk.
  4. Document everything for reasonable care. Keep emails, prior-notice confirmations, HTS worksheets, and supplier declarations. If the hold turns into a penalty, your reasonable care record is your defense.
